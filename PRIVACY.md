# BlockIDE Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** November 8, 2026
**Last Updated:** November 8, 2026

## 1. Introduction

BlockIDE ("we", "our", or "the Project") is an open-source development environment based on Visual Studio Code, designed to provide an integrated development experience for Minecraft-related projects.

We respect your privacy and are committed to minimizing the collection and processing of personal information.

This Privacy Policy explains how BlockIDE handles information when you download, install, or use our software.

## 2. Information We Collect

BlockIDE does not operate its own user tracking, advertising, or analytics infrastructure.

In particular, BlockIDE does not intentionally collect or maintain:

- Personal identification information.
- User account passwords.
- Source code or project files.
- Local workspace contents.
- Personal usage histories.
- Device location information.

BlockIDE does not sell users' personal information.

## 3. Local Data Storage

BlockIDE may store information locally on your device, including:

- Editor preferences and configuration.
- Installed extensions and extension settings.
- Workspace information.
- Cached files and application state.
- Authentication-related information managed by integrated extensions.

This information is generally stored within the application's local data directories and is not automatically transmitted to BlockIDE project maintainers.

## 4. Third-Party Services

BlockIDE integrates with or relies on third-party services to provide certain functionality.

These services may include:

- **Microsoft:** Microsoft account authentication and related services.
- **Minecraft-related services:** Services required by Minecraft development extensions.
- **Visual Studio Marketplace:** Extension discovery, installation, and updates.
- **GitHub:** Source code hosting and related developer services.
- **Other extension providers:** Services used by independently developed extensions.

When interacting with these services, information may be transmitted directly between your device and the relevant service provider.

These third parties operate under their own privacy policies. BlockIDE does not control their independent data collection practices.

## 5. Telemetry and Diagnostics

BlockIDE is based on Visual Studio Code and may inherit telemetry and diagnostic functionality from its upstream components.

Depending on the application version and configuration, these components may transmit:

- Application usage statistics.
- Error and diagnostic information.
- Crash reports.
- Performance-related information.

Third-party extensions may also implement independent telemetry mechanisms.

You can manage the upstream telemetry setting through:

`telemetry.telemetryLevel`

Setting this value to `off` disables VS Code telemetry reporting. However, this setting does not necessarily disable independent telemetry implemented by every third-party extension.

For more information, see:

https://code.visualstudio.com/docs/configure/telemetry

## 6. Data Sharing

BlockIDE does not sell, rent, or independently distribute personal information to advertisers or data brokers.

Information transmitted to third-party services is governed by the relevant service providers' privacy policies and the features you choose to use.

## 7. Data Security

We aim to minimize unnecessary data collection and avoid maintaining centralized databases containing users' personal information.

However, no software or internet transmission method can guarantee absolute security.

Users are responsible for maintaining the security of their devices and third-party accounts.

## 8. Children's Privacy

BlockIDE is a general-purpose development tool.

We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children through a dedicated user registration or data collection system.

## 9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when BlockIDE's functionality, architecture, or data handling practices change.

Updates will be published through the official BlockIDE project repository.

Continued use of BlockIDE after an updated policy takes effect constitutes use under the revised policy, subject to applicable law.

## 10. Contact

If you have questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact the BlockIDE maintainers through the official project repository.
